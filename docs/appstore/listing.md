**Description:**

BoxPilot turns your iPhone into a fast, modern remote for Infomir MAG set-top boxes, over your home Wi-Fi.

• A one-handed remote: large D-pad, volume and channel rockers, playback and colour keys, with a light haptic tap on every press.
• Phone volume buttons: change the TV volume without looking.
• Type on the TV: letters appear on the TV as you type. No more hunting across an on-screen keyboard for searches, URLs and logins.
• Touchpad: move the pointer, click and scroll on boxes that support a mouse.
• Macros: chain keys into one tap, like "back out to the menu" or "skip three channels".
• Quick actions: jump straight to favourite channels, or pin the keys you use most.
• Finds your box automatically. Works with boxes that use MAGic Remote pairing and boxes that use a Remote control password.
• Private by design: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Free to download with a 1-day free trial. After that, one purchase unlocks BoxPilot for good. No subscription.

Requires a MAG box with Remote control switched on (Settings → System settings → Remote control), on the same Wi-Fi as your iPhone.

BoxPilot is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Infomir. MAG is a trademark of Infomir.

Apple's guideline 3.1.1 requires the free-trial item to be a non-consumable named
"XX-day Trial" at the free price, and the app to say how long the trial lasts and
what happens after it. The paywall does both.

Each product needs a review screenshot (a screenshot of the paywall works) and
must be attached to the app version when you submit it.
