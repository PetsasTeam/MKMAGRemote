# BoxPilot Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

BoxPilot does not collect, store or share any personal data.

**What stays on your iPhone.** The MAG boxes you add (name, IP address, and the box password or pairing code), your macros and your shortcuts are saved only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

**Network use.** BoxPilot only talks to the MAG boxes on your own Wi-Fi network, to find them and send remote-control commands. It does not contact any server run by us.

**No tracking.** BoxPilot has no accounts, analytics, advertising, crash-reporting services or tracking of any kind.

**Purchases.** The free trial and the unlock are handled by Apple through the App Store. BoxPilot only learns whether the app is unlocked or in its free trial. Apple's privacy policy applies to the purchase itself.

**Local Network permission.** iOS asks for permission to find devices on your local network. It is used only to find and control your MAG box, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.

**Children.** BoxPilot is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
